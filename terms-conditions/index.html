Introduction

Welcome to our renovation services. By accessing our website, submitting an enquiry, requesting a consultation, or engaging our services, you agree to follow these Terms & Conditions. These terms are designed to create a clear, professional, and respectful experience for homeowners while helping us deliver our services effectively.


We may update these terms from time to time to reflect changes to our services, business practices, or applicable requirements. Any updated terms will apply from the date they are published.

Your Project, Clearly Defined

Every renovation is different. Before work begins, we aim to establish a clear understanding of your project, including the scope of work, design requirements, materials, timelines, responsibilities, and other relevant details.


Any project-specific terms, estimates, proposals, or agreements provided to you may contain additional information that applies specifically to your renovation.

Consultations & Project Enquiries

Consultations and enquiries help us understand your vision, property, requirements, and expectations. Information discussed during an initial consultation is intended to help determine the appropriate direction for your project.


Submitting an enquiry or requesting a consultation does not automatically create a contract or guarantee that a project will proceed. A renovation project will only begin once the relevant scope, pricing, terms, and required agreements have been accepted.

Estimates & Pricing

Renovation costs can vary depending on the size and condition of your property, materials, design selections, structural requirements, finishes, labour, and changes made during the project.


Any estimate or quotation provided is based on the information available at the time. Final pricing may change when project requirements, materials, site conditions, or requested modifications change. Where applicable, additional work or changes will be discussed before proceeding.

Project Changes

We understand that ideas can evolve as a renovation takes shape. If you request changes to the agreed scope, materials, finishes, layout, or other project elements, those changes may affect the overall cost and timeline.


We will communicate relevant changes as clearly as reasonably possible so you can make informed decisions about your project.

Scheduling & Timelines

We work carefully to plan each project around agreed requirements and availability. However, renovation timelines can sometimes be affected by factors outside our reasonable control, including material availability, supplier delays, inspections, permits, site conditions, weather, or changes requested during the project.


We will make reasonable efforts to keep you informed of significant changes that may affect your project schedule.

Homeowner Responsibilities

A successful renovation is a partnership. You are responsible for providing accurate information about your property and communicating relevant requirements, preferences, access arrangements, and project considerations.


Where required, you should also provide timely approvals, selections, decisions, or access necessary for the project to progress efficiently.

Quality, Materials & Craftsmanship

We aim to deliver work that reflects our standards for quality, functionality, and craftsmanship. Materials and products may vary slightly in colour, texture, grain, finish, or appearance due to their natural characteristics or manufacturing processes.


Where specific products or materials are selected, availability may affect the final selection. If a suitable alternative is required, we will communicate the relevant change where reasonably possible.

Safety & Site Standards

The safety of homeowners, visitors, workers, and other individuals on or around a renovation site is important to us. Appropriate safety practices and professional standards will be followed throughout the work.


Homeowners may be required to follow reasonable site-access, safety, and project instructions while renovation work is underway.

Payments & Project Costs

Payment schedules, deposits, balances, additional work, and other financial arrangements will be outlined in the applicable project agreement, quotation, or invoice.


You agree to make payments according to the agreed terms. Additional costs may apply where requested changes, unforeseen conditions, or additional work fall outside the original project scope.
